# Godly (uiuxshowcase.com/resources/godly-website), Whole-Page Composition Gallery

## What it is
A curated gallery of top-tier marketing, agency, and portfolio websites. Not a component library, a collection of full page compositions.

## What to extract
- Whole-page structure and pacing: how a page's sections are sequenced to build a narrative as the visitor scrolls, not just how individual components look.
- Hero treatment ideas at the composition level (scale, framing, what's above the fold) rather than component-level decoration.
- Scroll-driven interaction concepts: what reveals as you scroll, and in what order, to support a specific message rather than as generic ornamentation.

## When to use this
Reach for this when the task is a full landing page or marketing site and the question is "what should this page's overall shape and flow be," not "what should this one button look like."

## Caveats
A gallery of examples, not a documented system, extract composition *ideas*, not code. Cross-check anything visually decorative (gradients, glass panels, etc.) against the global CLAUDE.md design rules before using it; galleries like this often showcase exactly the patterns those rules ban.
